Why sellers choose us

A sourcing desk, not just freight forwarding

Most agents optimize either factory orders or parcel forwarding. We optimize the messy middle: many SKUs, small quantities, physical Shenzhen markets, and worldwide fulfillment.

Multi-SKU, low-MOQ

Combine 5–200 SKUs into one managed order. Each SKU can start from 5–20 pieces, ideal for phone cases, cables, chargers and accessories.

Huaqiangbei market runs

Our buyer walks the market for spot goods, negotiates at stall level, verifies versions and compatibility, and sends goods to our warehouse the same day.

Transparent landed cost

Every SKU shows goods cost, service fee, packaging, freight, duties and risk flags — no vague “one price” quotes.

SKU-level QC

We check counts, compatibility, accessories, packaging, weight, voltage, magnetism, battery risk and basic compliance before dispatch.

Market-ready prep

DDP shipping, FBA labels, TikTok Shop/Shopify prep, white-background photos and English listing assets can be added to any order.

Risk-first screening

Electronics and phone accessories are checked for refurbishment, trademark risk, plug type, battery handling and market-version mismatches.

FAQ

Common questions

What is the minimum order?

Most orders start at $100. Individual SKUs can begin from 5–20 units depending on supplier and product type.

Do you support Amazon FBA?

Yes. We can prepare FNSKU labels, cartons, packing lists and DDP delivery to FBA or your 3PL.

Can you buy from Huaqiangbei?

Yes. Market sourcing is one of our core services, especially for electronics, phone cases, cables, chargers and accessories.

How do you handle risk?

We flag battery, plug, magnet, wireless, trademark and refurbishment risks before you approve an order.
