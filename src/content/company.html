Company profile

A Shenzhen sourcing desk built for small, mixed orders.

SourcingRelay is a Shenzhen sourcing desk built on nearly two decades of 3C procurement, international small-B order support and Amazon operations experience.

We are an operator-run buying team rather than a marketplace or directory. Our work sits between the buyer and the supplier: finding options, checking versions and quantities, consolidating goods and flagging exceptions before shipment.

Our focus is the practical middle of sourcing — orders large enough to need coordination, but too mixed or too small to fit a traditional factory program comfortably.

Experience that shapes SourcingRelay

Every stage of our history changed how we run an order.

Our background is not a list of dates. More than 18 years across 3C export, Alibaba mixed-order sourcing and Amazon support directly shaped the checks and communication standards behind SourcingRelay.

Buyers need context, not just a quoted price.

Procurement, overseas sales and international trade shows taught us that model, material, packaging and intended market can change whether a product is right. SourcingRelay keeps those details visible from the first supplier comparison.

Small businesses need many items, not factory MOQs.

Supporting small-B buyers showed us how to source across suppliers, manage modest quantities and pack mixed phone-accessory and electronics orders into a workable shipment. That is the core of our service model today.

2019 · Amazon support → Retail readiness

Goods must arrive ready for the next step.

Procurement and packing work for recurring Amazon sellers sharpened our attention to carton planning, repeatability and long-term reliability — not only getting one order out of the door.

2026 · SourcingRelay → One accountable model

Experience becomes useful when it is visible.

Launching SourcingRelay brought these lessons into one direct brand: local supplier access, SKU-level evidence, transparent assumptions and honest communication when the original plan changes.

Candor is part of the service

What we will say no to.

Trust grows when a supplier draws clear lines before there is pressure to keep a shipment moving.

Before stock is confirmed

We will not promise a ship date.

We can share a target and explain what it depends on. We will not turn a hopeful date into a guaranteed one.

Before a certificate is verified

We will not call a product compliant.

Factory claims are not the same as evidence. Gaps go into the quote or risk review rather than disappear into a sales answer.

When a SKU is wrong

We will not hide the exception.

We hold the decision point and show you the issue, the available correction and the cost of each option.

Before a first payment

We will not route you to a personal account.

First payments use SourcingRelay’s company invoice and payment rails, with the order scope documented first.

Before you approve

Check the entity, the scope and the evidence.

  • Ask for the named team contact and a written quote scope.
  • Match the invoice beneficiary to the company details on your order.
  • Review assumptions, SKU exceptions and approvals before the next payment.
Payment safety

Only use our company account.

Treat any request to change bank details as a new instruction. Confirm it by a second channel before sending funds, and never send a first payment to a personal account.
