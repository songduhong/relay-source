Experience that shapes the work

What years on the supplier floor changed about SourcingRelay.

Our experience is not a list of adjectives. It determines what we check, what we put in writing and what we refuse to leave to the buyer.

Market buying → Sourcing choices

A product name is not a specification.

Years of comparing stalls and suppliers taught us that price means little until model, material, accessories and stock are confirmed. That is why supplier options now carry the version and assumptions beside the price.

QC work → Evidence

Photos alone do not prove a shipment.

Receiving-bench experience showed us that counts, compatibility and exceptions must be tied to individual SKUs. That is why our order record separates a useful QC trail from a generic photo gallery.

Export coordination → Cost clarity

A landed cost is not final until the details are.

Freight weight, packaging and destination rules can change the total quickly. Our logistics experience is why quotes show assumptions and changes before the buyer approves the next commitment.

Small-brand work → Accountable communication

Small teams need a person, not a ticket queue.

Growing sellers often lack a full sourcing department. We built the desk around an accountable order owner who turns operational detail into a clear decision, even when the answer is not what the buyer hoped for.

Candor is part of the service

What we will say no to.

Trust grows when a supplier draws clear lines before there is pressure to keep a shipment moving.

Before stock is confirmed

We will not promise a ship date.

We can share a target and explain what it depends on. We will not turn a hopeful date into a guaranteed one.

Before a certificate is verified

We will not call a product compliant.

Factory claims are not the same as evidence. Gaps go into the quote or risk review rather than disappear into a sales answer.

When a SKU is wrong

We will not hide the exception.

We hold the decision point and show you the issue, the available correction and the cost of each option.

Before a first payment

We will not route you to a personal account.

First payments use SourcingRelay’s company invoice and payment rails, with the order scope documented first.

Before you approve

Check the entity, the scope and the evidence.

  • Ask for the named team contact and a written quote scope.
  • Match the invoice beneficiary to the company details on your order.
  • Review assumptions, SKU exceptions and approvals before the next payment.
Payment safety

Use only the payment details on your issued invoice.

Treat any request to change bank details as a new instruction. Confirm it by a second channel before sending funds, and never send a first payment to a personal account.
