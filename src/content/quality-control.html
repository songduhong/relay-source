QC workflow

From supplier arrival to dispatch approval

1 · Receive & log

Each supplier delivery is weighed, logged and matched against the purchase order at our Shenzhen bench.

2 · Count check

Full piece count per SKU — not sampling. Shortages trigger an immediate re-buy or credit.

3 · Spec check

Connector type, plug standard, material, color, size and labeling are compared to your approved quote.

4 · Function test

Cables charge, chargers output, speakers pair, cases fit — each category has its own test checklist.

5 · Evidence pack

Photos and video per SKU are uploaded to your order workspace with pass/fail notes.

QC FAQ

Common QC questions

Is QC included in the service fee?

Yes. Standard count, spec and photo QC is included for every SKU. Complex function testing is quoted upfront.

Can I request extra photos?

Yes. Add photo or video requests to any SKU in your buy list and we include them at intake.

What happens if a SKU fails?

You choose: replace from another supplier, refund that SKU, or exclude it from the carton before dispatch.

Do you check for trademark risk?

We flag obvious brand logos and copyrighted designs. Final compliance decisions remain with you as the seller.
