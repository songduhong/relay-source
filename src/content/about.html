Payment FAQ

First-order questions

Do I pay suppliers directly?

No. First-time clients pay SourcingRelay under a managed order. We distribute payments to suppliers and keep purchase evidence in the workspace.

Can I split my first payment?

Many first orders can use a deposit plus pre-dispatch balance. The exact split is shown on your invoice before work starts.

What happens if a SKU fails QC?

We document the issue and propose replacement, supplier claim, price adjustment or exclusion from the shipment where possible.

Can I cancel before purchasing?

Yes. If no goods have been purchased, you can cancel before procurement begins. After purchase, unused funds may be eligible for replacement or refund under the order policy.
