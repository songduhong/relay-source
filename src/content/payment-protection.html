Milestone flow

How your money moves, step by step

You always know what has been paid, what it bought and what happens next — before the balance is due.

1 · Free quote

Send your buy list. You receive SKU-level pricing, landed cost and a risk flag — no payment needed to start.

2 · Protected deposit

Approve the quote and pay a managed deposit by Stripe, bank transfer or wire to our company account with a USD invoice.

3 · Documented buying

We pay suppliers and record each purchase with amount, supplier name and receipt in your workspace.

4 · QC evidence

Each SKU is counted, inspected and photographed. Problem SKUs are flagged with a correction path before dispatch.

5 · Dispatch approval

You review counts, photos and packing. Only after your approval do we collect the balance and ship.

If something goes wrong

Correction paths before your money is final

Issues found at QC are corrected before dispatch — not after your goods arrive.

Short count

If a SKU arrives short at QC, we re-buy from a second supplier at our cost or credit the shortfall against your balance.

Wrong spec

Wrong connector, plug type or material is returned to the supplier where possible, or replaced and re-inspected before dispatch.

Quality failure

Failed SKUs can be replaced, refunded or excluded from the carton — you decide with our recommendation before shipping.

Payment FAQ

Common payment questions

Do I pay suppliers directly?

No. You pay SourcingRelay under a managed order. We distribute payments to suppliers and keep the receipts in your workspace.

Can I pay by credit card?

Yes. Stripe supports major international cards. Bank transfer and wire are available for larger orders with lower fees.

When is the balance due?

Only after you approve QC photos, counts and packing. Nothing ships before your dispatch approval.

Do you accept PayPal friends & family?

No. We only accept traceable business payment methods — this protects both sides.
